PREMIUM EXCLUSIVE

Property Alerts for the areas you love.

New discoveries, delivered when they match your preferred areas. Property Alerts is included exclusively with Premium membership.

  1. Select your areas. Choose Myoko, Joetsu, Shinano Town / Kurohime and Iizuna, in any combination.
  2. Hear about new matches. Receive one daily email containing newly discovered properties that match your areas, only when there are new matching listings.
  3. Go straight to the source. Follow direct links from the email to the original Japanese property websites.
  4. Stay in control. Manage your preferred areas or disable notifications from your account page.
