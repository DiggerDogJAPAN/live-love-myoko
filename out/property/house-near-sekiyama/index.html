Overview

House for Sale – Built in 1977. This spacious 5-bedroom potential home—featuring 3 rooms upstairs and 2 downstairs—offers an excellent foundation for renovation and customization. With a generous kitchen, dining area, and living room, the layout provides plenty of space for family living or a future guesthouse project.

The property is designed for easy winter management, featuring a self-shedding roof and a well-fed water system that melts snow on the driveway as well as the snow that slides from the roof.

The large plot of land provides parking for 5 or more vehicles, plus there is a substantial storage/workshop building on-site that can be adapted for a variety of uses.

Located only 20 minutes from all major resorts, this property combines convenience, size, and great potential. With cleaning and renovation, it can become an excellent home or investment property in the Myoko area.
