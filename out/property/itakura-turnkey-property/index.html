Overview

Located in Itakura, Joetsu City, this substantial three-storey residence sits on 545.29 m² of land and provides approximately 190.31 m² of floor space.

Built in July 2000, the home has a 6SDK layout and a combination of timber and reinforced-concrete construction. Designed with Niigata's winters in mind, the property includes an integrated garage with space for three vehicles, as well as room for at least three additional vehicles within the grounds.

The house is all-electric and features central heating panels in individual rooms. Other practical features include a sunroom and balcony, while the substantial plot provides considerably more outdoor space than is typically available in the central Myoko resort villages.

Access is another strong point. The property fronts a wide 12-metre municipal road, including a pedestrian walkway, with approximately 18.8 metres of road frontage. The road is also identified as a snow-melting road, which is a valuable practical advantage during the region's heavy-snow winters.

Water is supplied by the public water system, with a private septic system for wastewater.

For buyers who do not need to be directly beside the lifts, the property offers an attractive alternative to resort-area real estate, with more house, land, parking and everyday practicality while keeping Myoko's ski areas within comfortable driving distance.
