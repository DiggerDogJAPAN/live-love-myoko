Overview

Located in Ōaza Okeumi, Myoko City, Niigata, this substantial property consists of two adjoining residential land parcels measuring 452.89 m² and 165.12 m², giving a combined registered land area of 618.01 m².

The residence extends over three storeys and has an impressive total registered floor area of 391.39 m². The basement area provides 145.70 m², the second floor 163.71 m², and the third floor 81.98 m². This generous scale provides plenty of scope for creating multiple living and entertaining areas, guest accommodation, storage, workspaces, or other uses depending on the buyer's plans and the necessary permissions.

Originally constructed in November 1984, the building is registered as a residential dwelling and uses a combination of timber and reinforced-concrete construction, topped with a stainless-steel sheet roof. Its substantial construction and large floor plan make it quite different from the smaller traditional homes commonly found around Myoko.

With more than 600 m² of residential land and nearly 400 m² of building space, the property represents an interesting opportunity for someone seeking a large Myoko base with room to renovate and adapt to their own requirements. Its scale could make it particularly attractive as a spacious family home, multi-generational residence, private ski property, or, subject to approvals and licensing, an accommodation-related project.

Area & Access

Although the property address falls within the wider Sekiyama area of Myoko City, it is important to note that the property is located up towards APA Resort, rather than in Sekiyama village itself. This is a quieter, elevated part of Myoko surrounded by the area's mountain and rural landscape, with APA Resort Joetsu Myoko nearby. The resort offers golf and seasonal leisure facilities, while the location also provides convenient access by car to the wider Myoko area. Sekiyama Station and local services are downhill from the property, while the ski areas and restaurants around Akakura, Ikenotaira and Myoko Kogen can be reached by car. The location is particularly suited to buyers looking for a peaceful Myoko base away from the busier resort villages, while remaining within driving distance of the area's main ski resorts and year-round attractions.
