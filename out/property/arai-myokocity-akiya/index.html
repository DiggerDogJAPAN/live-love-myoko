Overview

Spacious Family Home with Large Land in Central Myoko

Situated on a generous 687.59 m² (208 tsubo) block in a quiet residential area of Myoko City, this traditional Japanese home offers outstanding space and potential for renovation, investment, or family living.

The two-storey residence provides approximately 185 m² of internal living space with a practical 7DK layout, making it suitable for large families, multi-generational living, or conversion into guest accommodation (subject to approvals).

Although originally built in 1969 with an extension completed in 1985, the property has already benefited from kitchen and bathroom renovations, providing a solid foundation for further improvements.

Outside, the property includes two separate garages and a storage building, offering excellent parking and workshop space.

Located on a large parcel of land with very low annual property tax, this property represents an affordable opportunity in the Myoko area.
