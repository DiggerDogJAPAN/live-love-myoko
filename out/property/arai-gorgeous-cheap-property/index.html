Overview

Located in Arai, Myoko City, this substantial property sits across four adjoining residential land parcels with a combined registered area of 707.32 m² (approximately 214 tsubo).

The main house is a traditional two-storey timber residence providing approximately 230.96 m² of floor space, comprising 138.97 m² on the first floor and 91.99 m² on the second floor. The building dates from around 1947, making this a beautiful traditional older Japanese home.

The property property has been well renovated with modern kitchen, bathroom and double glazing! The new owner would need to do very little work. The house has some beautiful wooden features that you can only find in places like this.

In addition to the main residence, the property includes a separate two-storey timber outbuilding of approximately 39.66 m². 

The other attraction here is the sheer amount of space. With more than 700 m² of land, a large main residence and a separate outbuilding, there is plenty of flexibility for buyers looking for a substantial family home, workshop and storage space & gardens.

The property is being offered in its current condition, with some remaining contents to be removed. With an asking price in the region of ¥5 million, this represents an opportunity to acquire a very large beautiful house and substantial parcel of land in Myoko at an accessible price point.
