Overview

Located in the Arai area of Myoko City, this substantial three-storey residence offers 315.32 m² of floor space on a combined 342.41 m² of residential land.

Built in 2006, the property has a spacious 7LDK layout and a combination of timber and reinforced-concrete construction. The generous floor plan provides considerable flexibility for a large family, multi-generational living, guest rooms, home offices or recreational space.

The home was designed with the local climate in mind, featuring high ceilings, a stainless-steel snow-shedding roof and an integrated garage. The first-floor garage provides space for two standard vehicles or up to three smaller vehicles, offering valuable covered parking during Myoko's snowy winters.

The property is connected to public water and sewerage, city gas and Tohoku Electric Power. Road access is via an approximately 8-metre-wide prefectural road which is maintained for winter snow clearance.

With more than 315 m² of internal space, seven bedrooms and substantial covered parking, this is an unusually large residential property offering plenty of room for both everyday living and extended stays in Myoko.

Area & Access

Located in Arai, Myoko City, the property offers a practical year-round location away from the busier ski resort villages while maintaining easy access to the wider Myoko area. Everyday shops, services and transport connections are accessible by car, with Myoko's major ski areas within comfortable driving distance. The location is particularly attractive for buyers wanting a large permanent or holiday home with good road access and considerably more space than is typically available within the resort villages.
