Overview

Turnkey Ski-In/Ski-Out Chalet with Expansive Residential Land in Ikenotaira, Myoko

¥40,000,000 | 2,380 m² Land | 103 m² Chalet

Discover a unique opportunity to own a beautifully renovated ski-in/ski-out chalet set on an exceptionally large parcel of registered residential land in the sought-after Ikenotaira area of Myoko.

Located near the border of Ikenotaira and Suginosawa, at approximately 800 metres above sea level, this property combines the charm of a traditional Japanese mountain retreat with the convenience of a turnkey holiday home. With direct ski access to the surrounding resort, plus restaurants and natural hot springs within walking distance, it offers an ideal base for enjoying Myoko throughout the year.

Prime Ski-In/Ski-Out Location

One of the property's biggest attractions is its genuine ski-in/ski-out access to Ikenotaira Onsen Ski Resort, allowing owners and guests to enjoy the slopes directly from their mountain retreat without needing to drive.

The Alpenblick chairlifts are approximately a 15-minute walk away, while the resort restaurants and onsen hot spring facilities are also accessible on foot.

The surrounding area offers a peaceful mountain atmosphere while keeping some of Myoko's best winter attractions close at hand.

Renovated Three-Storey Ski Chalet

The property includes a distinctive three-storey chalet with approximately 103 m² of total floor space, arranged over three levels of roughly 34 m² each.

The interior has undergone extensive renovation, creating a comfortable, move-in-ready mountain home. Its vertical layout gives the chalet a cosy and distinctive character, making it well suited to use as a private ski retreat or seasonal holiday home.

With the major interior renovation work already completed, buyers can enjoy the property without immediately undertaking a substantial renovation project.

An Exceptional 2,380 m² Residential Landholding

What truly sets this property apart is its impressive land size.

The chalet sits on approximately 2,380 m² (720 tsubo) of registered residential land, a substantial holding in this desirable mountain location.

The generous grounds provide privacy, space and flexibility rarely found with smaller ski properties. The size of the land may also offer possibilities for additional accommodation, new buildings or future redevelopment, subject to local planning regulations, building requirements and any other applicable restrictions.

For buyers looking beyond a conventional ski cabin, the combination of an existing renovated chalet and a large residential landholding is particularly appealing.

Peaceful Mountain Setting and Winter Access

The property enjoys a quiet, private setting surrounded by Myoko's mountain environment.

During winter, vehicle access is available to a snow-cleared parking area at the end of the road, approximately a two-minute walk from the chalet. This arrangement provides a secluded snow-country atmosphere while maintaining practical access during the ski season.

The location is equally attractive outside winter, with Myoko's forests, hiking opportunities, hot springs and mountain scenery offering year-round enjoyment.

A Rare Opportunity in Myoko

Whether you're searching for a private ski retreat, a seasonal holiday home or a substantial landholding in one of Myoko's established resort areas, this property offers an unusual combination of location, space and convenience.

With its renovated three-storey chalet, genuine ski-in/ski-out access and expansive residential land, it represents an opportunity to secure a sizeable property in the heart of the Myoko mountain region.
