<p>For buyers looking beyond a conventional ski cabin, the combination of an existing renovated chalet and a large residential landholding is particularly appealing.</p>
<h3>Peaceful Mountain Setting and Winter Access</h3>
<p>The property enjoys a quiet, private setting surrounded by Myoko's mountain environment.</p>
<p>During winter, vehicle access is available to a snow-cleared parking area at the end of the road, approximately a two-minute walk from the chalet. This arrangement provides a secluded snow-country atmosphere while maintaining practical access during the ski season.</p>
<p>The location is equally attractive outside winter, with Myoko's forests, hiking opportunities, hot springs and mountain scenery offering year-round enjoyment.</p>
