Overview

This spacious two-storey home in Fujisawa, Nakago offers an unusually practical combination of generous living space, extensive storage and excellent winter infrastructure, making it particularly well suited to life in one of Japan's heaviest snowfall regions.

The property has been designed to accommodate a large family or two-household living, with separate kitchen and toilet facilities on both the first and second floors. Inside, the home combines Western-style living spaces with traditional Japanese character. A bright living and dining area, multiple Western-style rooms and authentic tatami rooms provide plenty of flexibility for family living, guests or creating dedicated work and recreation spaces.

One of the property's standout features is its comprehensive approach to winter snow management. A roof snow-melting system helps reduce the need for manual roof clearing, while road heating at the entrance helps keep the approach accessible during winter. These are particularly valuable features for anyone considering year-round ownership in the Myoko and Joetsu area.

Outside, the property sits on spacious grounds and includes multiple substantial outbuildings. There are concrete garages with shutter doors, additional outdoor parking and a two-storey warehouse offering considerable space for vehicles, skis, snow equipment, outdoor gear, tools or general storage.

The property is conveniently positioned for both Myoko and Joetsu. The Myoko Kogen ski areas are approximately 20 minutes away by car, while Joetsu Myoko Station provides access to the Hokuriku Shinkansen.

With its extensive space, two-floor living facilities, winter infrastructure and exceptional amount of storage, this is a distinctive property for anyone looking for a substantial year-round home or mountain base within easy reach of Myoko.

Area & Access

Nakago is a quiet residential and rural area conveniently positioned between central Joetsu and the mountains of Myoko. It offers a more relaxed local environment while remaining within practical driving distance of shops, everyday services and the Myoko Kogen ski areas. For winter, the location provides a useful alternative to living directly within the resort villages, with Myoko Kogen approximately 20 minutes away by car. Joetsu Myoko Station is also accessible for Hokuriku Shinkansen connections, making travel towards Tokyo and Kanazawa straightforward.
