A local partner for overseas owners

Ownership from a distance.
Care that stays close.

You should be able to enjoy your Myoko property without managing every detail from another country. We provide the ongoing presence, practical support and clear communication that make that possible.

01

Your property, checked regularly

Monthly internal and external inspections, with ventilation to help prevent mould and humidity damage. We look for small issues before they become larger problems.

02

A clear picture, wherever you are

Regular updates and photographs keep you informed about your home’s condition. Rental owners also receive transparent monthly performance statements.

03

Local help with the practical details

Key holding, bill payment and administrative support, plus liaison with neighbours and local authorities when you need someone on the ground.

Maintenance within management

The right people for the work.

When repairs are needed, we coordinate trusted local contractors for plumbing, electrical, heating and roofing work. Preventative maintenance advice supports the wider care of your property.

For holiday rentals & lodges

Your property.
Your guests. Our attention.

For owners welcoming paying guests, our management brings daily rental operations together with the ongoing oversight of your home.

01

Guests & arrivals

Booking enquiries, guest communication, in-person check-ins and 24/7 guest support. Pre-arrival checks cover heating, systems and clear access.

02

Cleaning & turnover

Professional cleaning, linen rental and restocking between stays, with guest rubbish and recycling handled according to local rules.

03

Compliance & reporting

Support with guest registries and fire safety standards, alongside transparent monthly owner statements and licensed Minpaku management.

04

Readiness & local response

Winterising systems, arranging snow clearing and preparing for the green season. Local assistance for boiler failures, leaks and lock-outs during guest stays.
