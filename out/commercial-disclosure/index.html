Services 販売する商品・サービス

Love Live Myoko provides online property search and discovery services focused on Myoko and surrounding areas in Japan.

Love Live Myoko offers two paid Property Finder membership levels, billed monthly:

Basic Membership

Provides access to the property search and discovery features included in the Basic membership plan, including the Property Finder directory, aggregated Japanese property listings, search filters and sorting, and direct links to original Japanese property websites.

Premium Membership

Provides everything included in Basic, with additional Property Finder features including eligible saved-property and Property Alerts functionality.

Property Alerts lets Premium members choose preferred areas and receive at most one daily email when new matching properties are discovered. Emails link directly to the original Japanese property websites. Members can manage their alert preferences or disable notifications through their account.

The exact features and limitations of each membership are displayed on the Finder Memberships page.

Basic and Premium memberships renew automatically each month unless cancelled. The subscription continues until cancelled or otherwise terminated.

Cancellation 解約について

Customers can manage and cancel their subscriptions through their account.

Cancellation stops future recurring subscription charges. Unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by applicable law, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period, and membership benefits remain available until that date.

To avoid a renewal charge, cancellation must be completed before the next scheduled payment is processed. If you experience difficulty cancelling, please contact us using the details above.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active subscription unless the account deletion process expressly confirms that the subscription has also been cancelled.

Refunds 返金について

Because Property Finder memberships provide access to digital services, subscription payments are generally non-refundable once access has been activated, except where required by applicable law or expressly stated in an applicable offer.

Cancelling a subscription does not ordinarily result in a refund for the remaining portion of the current billing period.

If we discontinue a paid service during an active subscription period or fail to provide a service as contracted, we address any refund or other remedy required by applicable law.

Premium members may qualify for a refund of up to three months of Premium membership fees following a qualifying property purchase through our agency. Eligibility is subject to the separately published conditions of the offer and verification; the refund is not automatic.

Refund requests and exceptions are handled in accordance with our Terms of Service and applicable law. Please contact us using the details above for refund enquiries.
